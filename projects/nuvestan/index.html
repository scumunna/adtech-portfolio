The Origin

I grew up in a household where nobody talked about money

I grew up in a household where nobody talked about money. No investments, no financial planning, no conversations about compound interest. When I started earning, I had to figure it all out from scratch. Budgeting apps showed me where my cash went. Investment apps let me buy stocks. But nothing connected the two -- nothing showed me what my spending habits were actually costing me in lost wealth over time.

Mint shows where money went. Robinhood lets you buy stocks. Neither helps you understand that the $342 you spent on dining this month could become over $200,000 in 20 years if invested instead.

That gap -- between spending awareness and wealth-building potential -- is what Nuvestan was built to close.
